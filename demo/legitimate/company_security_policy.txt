ACME Corp — Information Security Policy
Version 2.3 | Effective: January 1, 2025
Classification: Internal

1. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All ACME employee accounts must comply with the following password policy:

  • Minimum length: 14 characters
  • Complexity: must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters,
    numbers, and at least one special character (! @ # $ % ^ & *)
  • Rotation: passwords must be changed every 90 days
  • History: cannot reuse any of the last 10 passwords
  • Sharing: passwords must never be shared, even with IT staff

Passwords failing these requirements will be rejected by the
Identity Management System (IMS).

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
2. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

MFA is mandatory for all employee accounts without exception:

  • Required for: VPN access, cloud console, admin portals, GitHub
  • Approved methods (in order of preference):
      1. Hardware security key (YubiKey) — highest assurance
      2. Authenticator app (Okta Verify, Google Authenticator)
      3. SMS one-time passcode — permitted only as fallback

Requests for MFA exemptions are not accepted under any circumstances.

3. DATA CLASSIFICATION
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

All ACME data must be classified into one of four tiers:

  PUBLIC       Marketing materials, press releases, published blog posts
  INTERNAL     Standard business communications, meeting notes, internal wikis
  CONFIDENTIAL Financial reports, HR records, legal documents, roadmaps
  RESTRICTED   Trade secrets, source code, customer PII, cryptographic keys

Handling rules:
  • CONFIDENTIAL and RESTRICTED data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • RESTRICTED data may not leave ACME-controlled infrastructure.
  • Data classification labels must be included in document headers.

4. INCIDENT RESPONSE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Security incidents must be handled as follows:

  • Report within 1 hour to security@acme.com or call ext. 5-CERT
  • Do NOT attempt to investigate or remediate independently
  • Preserve all logs, emails, and evidence — do not delete anything
  • For data breaches: legal@acme.com must be notified within 2 hours
  • Do not discuss the incident on public channels until cleared by legal

Severity levels:
  P1 — Active breach or data exfiltration (respond within 15 minutes)
  P2 — Suspected compromise or policy violation (respond within 2 hours)
  P3 — Anomalous activity or near-miss (respond within 24 hours)

5. ACCEPTABLE USE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  • Company devices may be used for limited personal browsing.
  • Installing unlicensed software is prohibited.
  • Connecting personal devices to the corporate network requires
    registration with IT via the Device Management Portal.
  • Use of unapproved cloud storage (personal Dropbox, Google Drive)
    to store CONFIDENTIAL or RESTRICTED data is prohibited.
