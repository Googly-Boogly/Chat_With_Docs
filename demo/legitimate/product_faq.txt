PRICING & PLANS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: How much does AcmeCloud cost?
A: AcmeCloud is offered in two paid tiers:
   • Pro:        $149/month per workspace (billed monthly)
                 $119/month per workspace (billed annually — saves 20%)
   • Enterprise: $499/month base (billed monthly), unlimited workspaces
                 Custom annual contracts available — contact sales@acme.com

Q: Is there a free trial?
A: Yes. All new accounts receive a 14-day free trial with full Pro-tier features.
   No credit card is required to start the trial. At the end of the trial you
   choose a paid plan or your data is retained for 30 days before deletion.

Q: Are there non-profit or educational discounts?
A: Yes. Verified non-profits and educational institutions receive an additional
   30% discount on top of all listed prices. Contact billing@acme.com with
   your 501(c)(3) documentation or .edu email domain for verification.

Q: What happens if I exceed my plan limits?
A: We'll notify you by email at 80% and 100% of your limits. Usage over the
   limit is billed at a metered rate detailed in your plan agreement.

FEATURES & LIMITS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: How many users can I add to a Pro workspace?
A: Pro plans include up to 25 seats. Adding seats beyond 25 requires an
   upgrade to the Enterprise plan or a seat add-on at $12/seat/month.

Q: What file storage does each plan include?
A: Pro: 100 GB per workspace. Enterprise: 5 TB base, expandable on request.

Q: What integrations are supported?
A: AcmeCloud integrates natively with:
   Slack, GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Confluence, Salesforce, HubSpot,
   Zendesk, PagerDuty, and Datadog.
   Additionally, our REST API and webhook system support custom integrations
   with any platform. API documentation: docs.acme.com/api

SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT (SLA)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: What uptime do you guarantee?
A: Uptime guarantees depend on the plan:
   • Pro:        99.5% monthly uptime (approx. 3.6 hours downtime/month max)
   • Enterprise: 99.9% monthly uptime (approx. 43 minutes downtime/month max)

   SLA credits apply if we fall below these thresholds. See acme.com/sla.

Q: What support options are available?
A: • Pro:        Email support, 24-hour response time during business days
   • Enterprise: 24/7 phone, chat, and email support with 1-hour response SLA
                 Dedicated customer success manager for accounts > $10k/year

DATA & SECURITY
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Q: Where is my data stored?
A: Data is stored in AWS us-east-1 by default. Enterprise customers can
   request EU (eu-west-1) or APAC (ap-southeast-1) residency at no extra cost.

Q: Is AcmeCloud SOC 2 certified?
A: Yes. AcmeCloud holds SOC 2 Type II certification. Our audit report is
   available under NDA to Enterprise customers. Contact security@acme.com.

Q: Can I export all my data?
A: Yes. A full data export (JSON + CSV) can be triggered from Account Settings
   at any time. Enterprise customers can also use the Data Export API.
